NLP in Practice: From Bag-of-Words to LLMs

An industry perspective on how language models evolved, and how each idea is used in real products today.

⏱ 120 minutes 🧪 Live Colab demos 🏭 Real-world case studies
Running thread: customer reviews and support tickets from an Indian e-commerce or food-delivery app. Every section solves the same problem with a newer technique, so the class sees why each idea was invented, not just what it is.

Opening hook

  • DemoRun one messy Hinglish review ("delivery late tha but food ekdum mast 🔥") through a 2005-style keyword filter and then a modern LLM. The gap between them is what the session explains.

Fast-forward foundations

  • The NLP pipeline in a real system: tokenization, normalization, stemming vs lemmatization, POS tagging, NER.
  • Why production text is hard: typos, code-mixing, emojis, domain jargon.
  • Feature extraction in 5 minutes: BoW, n-grams, TF-IDF, Naive Bayes.
  • IndustryTF-IDF + logistic regression is still the baseline every team must beat.
  • DemoSupport-ticket classifier in ~10 lines.

25 min

Word embeddings: the big leap

  • Why one-hot fails → "you know a word by the company it keeps."
  • word2vec (CBOW, skip-gram, negative sampling), GloVe, fastText subwords for typos and Hinglish.
  • DemoGensim: king − man + woman, nearest neighbours, bias in embeddings.
  • IndustrySearch query expansion; product/listing embeddings for recommendations (item2vec).
  • Limitation: one vector per word ("bank") → sets up context-aware models.

RNNs, LSTMs & GRUs

  • RNNs as language models, and how they relate to n-grams.
  • Vanishing gradients, shown visually.
  • LSTM and GRU gates as "memory with a forget button."
  • DemoLSTM sentiment model or character-level text generation.
  • IndustryKeyboard next-word prediction, speech, time series.
  • Why they were replaced: sequential processing and weak long-range memory.
